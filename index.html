Does the caller know it is an AI?
Yes. The first sentence discloses that it is an AI receptionist for the business. We do not pretend it is a human, and the agent confirms it plainly if asked.
Will it quote prices or promise arrival times?
No. It captures the job and says the team will confirm the price. It will not promise that someone is on their way unless you have given it an explicit SLA.
What happens in an emergency?
It asks whether anyone is in immediate danger, gives the right emergency guidance — 999, or the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 for a suspected gas leak — captures the details and flags the call for a fast callback.
Do I have to change my number?
No. You keep the number that is on your van and your listings, and forward calls to Relay when you want it to answer — always, after hours, or only when you are busy.
Can it book jobs in my calendar?
Relay captures the request and gives you a callback link. Calendar and job-management integrations are on the roadmap and scoped per customer.
Are calls recorded?
Audio recording is off by default. Transcripts and summaries are stored so you can review the job. Read our privacy notice for retention and your customers’ rights.
