Privacy notice

Last updated 7 November 2026 · Version 1.0

The short version: we use business contact details to reply to enquiries and run demos. We do not sell data. Audio recording is off by default. If you are a customer of a trade business that uses Relay, that business controls your call data — please contact them first.

Who we are

Relay is an AI receptionist service for UK trade businesses, operated by Umair Zahid. In this notice, “Relay”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean that service.

For privacy questions or requests, email aumair525@gmail.com. We aim to reply within one month.

Relay is the controller of personal data used for this website, our own enquiries and our marketing. When a trade business uses Relay to answer its calls, that business is the controller of its customers’ call data and Relay acts as its processor. We handle that data only on the business’s instructions and under a data processing agreement.

What we collect

WhoWhatWhy
Website visitors IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of request in standard server logs. To serve the site, keep it secure and diagnose faults. No analytics or advertising cookies are used.
People who book a demo Name, email address, company, and anything you choose to write in the booking form. Bookings are handled by Cal.com. To hold the demo and follow up about the service.
Prospects and clients Business contact details, email correspondence, call notes and commercial records. To respond to enquiries, provide the service and keep business records.
Callers to a client’s business Name, phone number, postcode, job details, urgency, preferred time, transcript and call metadata — captured on behalf of the client. To answer the call, capture the job and arrange a callback or visit for the client.

We do not intentionally collect special-category data (such as health, religion or ethnicity), financial account details or data about children. Please do not include that information in a booking form or call.

Lawful basis

  • Legitimate interests — replying to business enquiries, promoting Relay to relevant UK trade businesses, keeping the website secure and improving the service. We have balanced those interests against your rights.
  • Contract — providing a demo or the service you or your business have asked for.
  • Legal obligation — keeping records where the law requires it, or responding to a lawful request.
  • Consent — where we ask for it specifically. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Where we process call data for a client, the client is responsible for identifying its lawful basis and giving its callers the relevant privacy information. We provide the client with a caller privacy notice template and a data processing agreement.

Who we share it with

We use service providers to run Relay. They process data on our behalf or, for Cal.com, as an independent provider when you book a demo:

  • Oracle Cloud — hosting this website and its server logs.
  • Cal.com — demo booking and scheduling. Cal.com’s own privacy policy applies to the booking page.
  • Vapi, Twilio, OpenAI and Deepgram — voice-agent, telephony, language-model and speech-to-text processing, only when a client uses the service.
  • Email and IT providers — correspondence, backups and support.
  • Professional advisers — accountants, lawyers and insurers where necessary.

We do not sell personal data. We may disclose it if required by law, a regulator or a court, or to protect someone’s safety.

International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where that happens, we use an appropriate transfer mechanism (such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum) and require appropriate contractual protections. Contact us if you want more detail.

How long we keep it

  • Website server logs: up to 30 days, unless needed to investigate a security incident.
  • Demo and prospect records: up to 12 months after the last meaningful contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Client call records: as set out in the client’s DPA. The default is 90 days for transcripts, with longer retention only for an open complaint or legal claim.
  • Do-not-contact records: kept indefinitely so we can honour your request.
  • Financial and legal records: as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You have the right to be informed, to access a copy of your data, to ask us to correct or erase it, to restrict or object to processing, and to ask for portability where it applies. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To make a request, email aumair525@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting. We will respond within one month, and may extend that period if the request is complex.

If you are a customer of a trade business that uses Relay, please contact that business first — it controls your call data. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office:

  • ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
  • 0303 123 1113
  • Information Commissioner’s Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF

Security

We use HTTPS, access controls and reputable service providers. We keep the data we hold to what is needed. We do not use client call data to train AI models, and we do not sell it. No system is completely secure; if you think your data has been compromised, contact us immediately.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It loads its fonts and styles locally. When you choose to open the Cal.com booking page, Cal.com may set cookies or similar technologies on its own site; its privacy policy explains that.

Changes

We may update this notice. The date and version at the top will change. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will take reasonable steps to let you know.
