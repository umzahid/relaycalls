Questions

The things owners ask.

Do I have to give you my Google password?

No. You invite me as a Manager on your Google Business Profile by email. You can remove me at any time, and I never see your login.

How fast is it?

72 hours from the moment I have access and your approval. The approval step is usually the slow part, so reply quickly if you want it done that week.

What if I don't like a reply?

You approve every word before anything is posted. Edit anything you like, or ask for a rewrite. One round of edits is included in the sprint.

Do you write fake reviews?

No, never. Fake and incentivised reviews breach Google's policies and UK consumer protection law — and they get profiles suspended. I only reply to real reviews, and I never filter out unhappy customers.

Can you guarantee more calls or a higher rating?

No. Anyone who guarantees Google rankings or review scores is lying to you. I guarantee the work, the deadline and that every reply is honest. The rest depends on your business.

Where are you based?

We're a remote team working with UK trade businesses. Everything is handled by email, and you approve every word before it goes live. If remote is a dealbreaker, that's fair — no hard feelings.

What happens after the sprint?

Nothing unless you want it to. If it was useful, the retainer is £99/month for up to 10 replies and 4 posts. If not, you keep the review kit and the report and we part on good terms.
