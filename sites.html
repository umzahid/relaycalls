Questions

No. We can launch on a free subdomain and connect your domain later. If you already have one, we point it at the new site — you keep ownership.

Yes. Send what you have. If the photos are poor, we use clean stock images and tell you which shots would improve the page.

One page with sections: hero, services, areas, reviews, contact. That is deliberate — it is faster and converts better on a phone.

Yes. We write it from your services and reviews, then you approve every word. No invented claims.

No. We guarantee the site, the speed and the deadline. Calls depend on your area, your prices and your work.

You do. We hand over the files and the domain access at launch.
